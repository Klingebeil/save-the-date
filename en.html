GETTING THERE

By car

Gut Kaltenbrunn is easily accessible by car and is located just outside of Gmund. There is plenty of parking available on site and parking is free for guests.

By public transport

The easiest way to reach Gut Kaltenbrunn from Munich is by taking the RB57 train towards Tegernsee. From the station, it is about 15 minutes on foot or 10 minutes by bus 359 to the "Kaltenbrunn B. Gmund" stop.

HOTELS

Hotels in Gmund may be fully booked early in advance for August. Please reserve a room in good time if you wish to stay overnight close to the venue.
